## Recommended Strategy: Two-Track Approach

**Track A (immediate, low-stakes, feedback-gathering):**
Submit a **non-archival 2–4 page extended abstract** to **CMCL 2026** by Feb 25. This:
- Gets expert eyes on the work in the exact right community
- Doesn't lock up the full paper (non-archival)
- Gives presentation experience at an international venue (Palma, Mallorca in May)
- Costs very little effort — the data is ready, just need to write 2–4 focused pages
- LREC conference registration is typically €300–500

**Track B (the real paper, no deadline pressure):**
Write the full paper at own pace and submit to **TACL** (rolling deadline, 1st of any month). If accepted, present at ACL or EMNLP. If the reviews are useful but it's rejected, revise and either resubmit to TACL or redirect to EMNLP/ICLR 2027.

The CMCL abstract (non-archival, 2–4 pages) would cover:
- 1 page: intro + hypothesis
- 1 page: method (Mosteller data, 4 syntactic types, ridge regression, 5 models)
- 1 page: key results (within-type ρ > 0.90, Vogel cross-validation ρ = 0.991, truncation)
- 0.5 page: discussion + significance
